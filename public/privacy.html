Privacy notice

Draft. The final wording will be published before launch.

What stays on your phone

NANDI keeps your tasks, workouts, answers and settings in the app's storage on this phone. They are not sent to us.

In the Android app, automatic backup is on unless you turn it off. Once a day it saves a copy as a plain, readable file in Documents/NANDI/auto on this phone, and keeps the last 7. Other apps that can read your Documents folder can read these files. You can turn this off in You, then Backup.

With Usage access on, the Android app reads which app is in front for App pause, Phone ki chhutti and the focus check (during a focus, a guarded app like Instagram pauses the timer). It reads this on the phone and keeps nothing of it but the minutes it needs; none of it is sent to us.

What leaves it

The app checks our server for updates. That check carries none of your activity. The automatic copies stay in Documents on this phone. A backup or a share leaves the phone only when you send or move it yourself.

Removing your data

Removing the app or clearing its data erases the app's own storage. The automatic copies in Documents/NANDI/auto stay on the phone, so you can restore later. To remove them too, delete that folder with your phone's Files app.

Age

NANDI is for people aged 18 or older.
