# Chiku Psychology scene review

Ten draft scenes cover all ten Psychology ideas with at least two misconceptions. Each scene has three cards, and both misconception cards lead Chiku to an answer different from the scene's truth. `claimIds` beside each scene point to the concept claims used for its truth.

| Idea | Right card | Misconception cards (IDs) | Prop | How the new day separates the cards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Habits | The train leaving | Thirty days make it stick (`habits-m1`); Wanting to study starts it (`habits-m3`) | bus | The train cue disappears while Noor still has time and wants to study. The cue card says no app; both other cards say yes. |
| Loss aversion | No loss from her start | Gains and losses feel equal (`loss-aversion-m1`); The starting point changes nothing (`loss-aversion-m3`) | coin | Leila receives a sure $10 before the same $20-or-$0 toss. Losing that $10 makes her decline; both other cards keep her taking the toss. |
| Anchoring | First numbers pull bids | Random numbers have no pull (`anchoring-m2`); One check erases the pull (`anchoring-m4`) | shop | The $100 number is random and Maya lists reasons against it. A small pull remains; the other cards erase it. |
| Confirmation bias | An early bus could disprove it | One match proves it (`confirmation-bias-m1`); Many matches prove it (`confirmation-bias-m3`) | bus | One early bus breaks Omar's claim about every bus. The matching-case cards still keep the claim. |
| Social proof | A group can be mistaken | The crowd proves it (`social-proof-m1`); Everyone follows the crowd (`social-proof-m4`) | crowd | A clear platform sign conflicts with another group's answer. Eva follows the sign; both other cards keep her with the crowd. |
| Planning fallacy | Past tasks reveal missed time | The best case is normal (`planning-fallacy-m1`); Past tasks cannot help (`planning-fallacy-m2`) | clock | The next poster has a printer step that took an extra hour last time. Theo starts early; both other cards keep his first time. |
| Sleep and memory | Rest helps keep new learning | Staying up works better (`sleep-memory-m3`); Busy breaks help more (`sleep-memory-m5`) | bed | After a new word list, Inez can sleep or stay busy until the quiz. The rest card sends her to bed; the other cards keep her awake. |
| Variable rewards | The next prize is uncertain | Only willpower keeps him going (`variable-rewards-m3`); Steady prizes last longer (`variable-rewards-m6`) | coin | Sam is tired after three empty tries but still cannot tell when a prize will come. The uncertain prize keeps him trying; the other cards end his turn. |
| If-then plans | The named bus is her cue | A wish is enough (`if-then-plans-m1`); One sharp cue costs nothing (`if-then-plans-m3`) | notebook | The named bus cue is gone, though Hana has time at home. The cue card leaves the notebook shut; both other cards open it. |
| Spotlight effect | Her own view feels larger | Everyone noticed it (`spotlight-effect-m1`); Nobody ever notices (`spotlight-effect-m2`) | notebook | Zoya checks recall in a new group. One of six recalls the shirt, so she writes “some”; both all and none miss it. |

Skipped ideas: none.

These are specific, staged outcomes that illustrate the cited patterns. The population findings in the pack do not guarantee any named person's choice, bid, recall, or memory result.
