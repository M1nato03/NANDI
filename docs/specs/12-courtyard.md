# 12. The courtyard (aangan) and the Sunday storybook

Owner ask (2026-10-21): the cast must not copy Duolingo's cheering mascot. Every current feature stays.
Duolingo's mascot works for the app (it pulls you back). Ours points at your life: the friends show what you
really did, they grow with the part of life they look after, and nothing ever shrinks or looks sad.

## 1. Friends and their areas

Each friend looks after one area. A **practice day** is a day with at least one real fact in that area's ledger.

| Friend | Area | Practice day = a ledger fact of | Corner objects, one per stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nandi** | Train | `train` session or rest check | mat, wooden clubs (mudgar), dumbbells, pull-up bar, medal hook |
| **Mor** | Plan | `tasks` done, or a kept Phone ki chhutti (spec 24 #1; each kept day also hangs one marigold on the door toran) | marigold pot, second pot, flower bed, creeper trellis, small water bowl |
| **Chiku** | Learn | `mind` learn-answer from the daily drop, learn-drop, lesson | book, book stack, low shelf, reading lamp, globe |
| **Moti** | Remember | `mind` learn-retention, card, or an answer from a night or practice drop | keepsake box, photo frame, jar shelf, trunk, photo string with lights |
| **Kapi** | Mind | `mind` board or mystery | puzzle cube, chess stool, kite, telescope, slate of sums |
| **Kachhua** | Sleep | `lightsout` with metTarget | sleeping mat, pillow, charpai (woven cot), lantern, blanket |

The courtyard itself (the neem tree in the middle) grows with **active days**: days with any practice in any area.

## 2. Growth

- Stages by practice days: 0 → stage 0; 3 → 1; 7 → 2; 14 → 3; 30 → 4; 60 → 5 (max). Tree uses the same table
  on active days. Each stage adds one object to the friend's corner; nothing is ever removed.
- Growth only counts days, never XP, so it cannot be farmed in one sitting and a light day counts as much as a big one.
- No decay. A missed day changes nothing; a rest day or day away is simply a quiet day.
- Everything is derived from the ledger. Nothing new is stored, so backups and restores need no change.

## 3. The scene

- Shown at the top of Plan (tap → full courtyard screen) and in full at `you/courtyard`.
- Sky follows the clock: **morning** 04:00–11:00, **day** 11:00–shutdown, **evening** shutdown–bedtime,
  **night** bedtime–04:00 (the adaptive busy-day shift applies).
- Friend pose: done in its area today → cheer; not yet → idle (morning/day) or dozing (evening). At night every
  friend sleeps and the scene is dark and calm.
- Words are plain and kind: "Nandi trained with you today", never "Nandi misses you".

## 4. The Sunday storybook

- A short picture story of one week (Monday–Sunday), on Plan on Sundays and Mondays, and in You → Storybooks
  for the last 8 weeks.
- Pages: cover (week dates, the courtyard), one page per area practised that week (friend, its corner,
  a hand-written line using the real count), one closing page. A week with no practice gets a single gentle page.
- Lines are hand-written per area and per band (1 day, 2–4 days, 5+ days), two variants each, picked by week.
- No share step yet (the weekly recap already has a share card).

## 5. Not doing

No pet that needs feeding, no sad or hungry states, no loss, no random rewards, no Duolingo names or art.
