# Learning apps: mechanics and evidence

**Duolingo.** Settles and Meeder’s [Half-Life Regression](https://research.duolingo.com/papers/settles.acl16.pdf) (2016) estimated when a learner would forget a word or concept so practice could be timed accordingly; their study reported over 45% less recall-prediction error than several baselines and 12% higher daily engagement in an operational test. Duolingo’s [Birdbrain](https://blog.duolingo.com/unique-engineering-problems/) (2024) uses exercise responses to estimate concept proficiency and select practice difficulty. [Streaks](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-streaks-keep-duolingo-learners-committed-to-their-language-goals/) (2017), weekly XP [leagues](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-leagues-leaderboards/) (2023), and [reminder notifications](https://blog.duolingo.com/lessons-from-asia-turning-local-research-into-global-experiments/) (2021) encourage return visits. In its latest published quarter, Q2 2026, Duolingo reported **58.7 million daily active users, 12.7 million paid subscribers, and $298.5 million revenue**; full-year 2025 revenue was **$1.038 billion**. [Q2 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm) (2026); [FY2025 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012246/q4fy25duolingo12-31x25shar.htm) (2026).

**Learning versus engagement.** A [2022 qualitative study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.16175) found cases where users pursued game rewards at the expense of learning; it cannot establish how common that is. A [2024 study](https://cdnprodwpv2.avantassessment.com/wp-content/uploads/The-effectiveness-of-Duolingo-in-developing-receptive-and-productive-language-knowledge-and-proficiency.pdf) found significant reading, listening, writing, and speaking gains after about 27 hours of Duolingo Spanish among 48 learners. Duolingo funded that study, which did not establish advanced proficiency or compare the app with an alternative course. The fair reading is that short app practice can help beginners, while a streak or league rank is **not a proficiency measure**. [Study](https://cdnprodwpv2.avantassessment.com/wp-content/uploads/The-effectiveness-of-Duolingo-in-developing-receptive-and-productive-language-knowledge-and-proficiency.pdf) (2024).
