# Making “less phone” feel desirable

**Why scrolling has social pull.** For young people, the phone is often where friendships are maintained and group norms are visible. A 2024 study found that perceived peer norms shape how adolescents use social media and experience friendship; another linked online connection with friendship closeness. Seen through social identity and signalling theory, staying online says “I’m part of this,” while an offline choice needs its own visible social meaning. That is an inference from the studies, not evidence that every young person feels the same way. ([2024 peer-norm study](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1222907); [2024 friendship study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/developmental-psychology/articles/10.3389/fdpys.2024.1419756/full))

**The strongest shift is from restraint to belonging.** Strava makes effort legible through segments and kudos: it reported over **10 billion kudos** in 2023, then a **59% rise in running-club participation** in 2024. Its surveys also found friendship was a reason to join fitness groups. Those figures show participation and social proof, not that kudos caused the growth. ([Strava, 2024](https://press.strava.com/articles/strava-releases-year-in-sport-trend-report); [Strava, 2024 report](https://press.strava.com/zi/articles/strava-releases-annual-year-in-sport-trend)) Nike Run Club offers free guided runs and neighbourhood challenges, with roughly **300 audio-guided runs** available in 2024: it gives beginners a credible “runner” identity before they are fast. ([Nike, 2024](https://about.nike.com/en/newsroom/releases/nike-run-club-app-new-features))

**Personality and shared rituals make earnest habits easier to display.** Duolingo’s absurd, insistent owl made language practice a joke friends could join; its *Friend Streak* turned a private habit into mutual encouragement. Duolingo reported that one-third of daily active users had a Friend Streak and that its 2024 Instagram Reels views rose over **450%** year on year; it says viral marketing contributed to growth, without isolating its effect. ([Duolingo, 2024](https://blog.duolingo.com/friend-streak/); [Duolingo shareholder letter, 2024](https://duolingo.gcs-web.com/static-files/99006c40-d8cf-41ca-b5b1-c5cb1fa5ba88)) Spotify Wrapped applies the same principle to identity: attractive, personal cards are built to share, and Spotify reported **10% higher year-on-year engagement** for its 2024 campaign. A “time well spent” recap could make offline pursuits similarly easy to celebrate. ([Spotify, 2024](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2024-12-04/wrapped-user-experience-2024/); [Spotify, 2024 results](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/news-and-insights/spotify-fourth-quarter-earnings-2024/))

**There are several versions of an offline status signal.** BeReal made unfiltered daily moments with friends the point; its buyer reported over **40 million active users** in 2024, though research found that “authentic” posting could still feel restrictive or toxic. ([Voodoo, 2024](https://voodoo.io/news/voodoo-acquires-bereal); [adolescent study, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02883)) WHOOP makes recovery and sleep discussable through team comparisons; Peloton pairs instructors, a leaderboard and friend-finding, reporting **6.4 million members** in 2024. Neither figure establishes that social features produced better habits. ([WHOOP, 2025](https://community.whoop.com/t/about-the-community-teams-category/14); [Peloton, 2024](https://investor.onepeloton.com/static-files/ade7b913-a61e-4e85-9dc5-a8ceb7c12f11))

GymTok, “monk mode,” “touch grass,” and *build in public* give exercise, focus, outdoor time and incremental work recognizable names or stories people can share. They also show the tension: the offline act may acquire status through an online post. ([fitness-tracker interviews, 2024](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/13634593231175277); [World Economic Forum, 2023](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2023/02/monk-mode-boost-productivity/); [Google Trends, 2026](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/touch-grass-trends/); [Indie Hackers discussion, 2024](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/building-in-public-may-be-losing-its-shine-as-indie-hackers-question-its-value-6NIWwx3XNNlJxSSLnjbJ))

**From 2024–26, the object itself became a signal.** Light Phone presented a deliberately limited, premium-looking phone; Brick and Unpluq use physical objects to pause access to distracting apps; Opal gives focus a score and shareable social features. Opal reported **500,000 daily users** and **150 million hours saved** in 2024—company calculations, not an independent trial. Brooklyn’s teen *Luddite Club* made flip phones and in-person meetings a peer identity; luxury travellers were also more likely to cite digital detox as a reason to travel. ([Light Phone, 2024](https://www.thelightphone.com/blog/light-iii-design-manifesto); [Brick, 2026](https://getbrick.com/); [Unpluq, 2025](https://www.unpluq.com/faq); [Opal, 2024](https://opalapp.com/blog/keynote-01-opals-year-in-focus-2024); [Brooklyn Public Library, 2023](https://www.bklynlibrary.org/podcasts/teens-are-offline); [McKinsey, 2024](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/travel/our-insights/updating-perceptions-about-todays-luxury-traveler))

**For young Indians, make the offline choice locally social and affordable.** Google/Kantar found **79%** of Indian Gen Z respondents used Google or YouTube daily; Indian reporting in 2025 described run clubs attracting young people through friendship and shared experiences. That supports creator-led invitations and neighbourhood groups more directly than an expensive hardware-only approach. ([Google/Kantar, 2024](https://business.google.com/in/think/consumer-insights/gen-z-trends-search-behaviour-india/); [Mint, 2025](https://www.livemint.com/mint-lounge/wellness/gen-z-fitness-trends-running-pickleball-strength-training-pilates-hyrox-11754381537979.html))

**Where status games backfire:** leaderboards can turn effort into comparison; streaks can turn a missed day into shame; GymTok’s idealized bodies can motivate while also undermining body image. BeReal’s authenticity research found the same risk in a different form. ([fitness-tracker study, 2024](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/13634593231175277); [BeReal study, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02883))

**Ten tactics to test**—design proposals drawn from the cited cases, not proven outcomes:

1. Show **what time made possible**: a walk, song practised or friend met, instead of only minutes avoided. ([Spotify, 2024](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2024-12-04/wrapped-user-experience-2024/))
2. Offer beautiful, optional weekly recap cards with no screen-time total exposed. ([Spotify, 2024](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2024-12-04/wrapped-user-experience-2024/))
3. Let friends send quiet appreciation for an activity, with no public ranking. ([Strava, 2024](https://press.strava.com/articles/strava-releases-year-in-sport-trend-report))
4. Create small friend commitments, such as a shared phone-free meal. ([Duolingo, 2024](https://blog.duolingo.com/friend-streak/))
5. Reward returning after a break as warmly as keeping a streak. ([BeReal study, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02883))
6. Give groups distinctive names, visuals and invitations to local meetups. ([Strava, 2024](https://press.strava.com/zi/articles/strava-releases-annual-year-in-sport-trend))
7. Let people share a work in progress, with visibility chosen by them. ([Indie Hackers, 2024](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/building-in-public-may-be-losing-its-shine-as-indie-hackers-question-its-value-6NIWwx3XNNlJxSSLnjbJ))
8. Make starting focus tactile and stylish, while keeping access practical. ([Light Phone, 2024](https://www.thelightphone.com/blog/light-iii-design-manifesto); [Brick, 2026](https://getbrick.com/))
9. Use playful, locally relevant language rather than “addiction” labels. ([Duolingo, 2024](https://duolingo.gcs-web.com/static-files/99006c40-d8cf-41ca-b5b1-c5cb1fa5ba88); [Google/Kantar, 2024](https://business.google.com/in/think/consumer-insights/gen-z-trends-search-behaviour-india/))
10. Let users choose private progress, friend support or public sharing—and leave any comparison easy to switch off. ([peer-norm study, 2024](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1222907); [BeReal study, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02883))
