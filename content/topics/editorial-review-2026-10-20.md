# W3 topic editorial review — 2026-10-20

Each topic has one module, eight concepts and 64 items. Each concept has two seed-1, four seed-2 and two seed-3 items. At seeds 2 and 3, choice items have at least three plausible options with separate reasons for each wrong choice. Every estimate asks for a source-backed quantity. Health text is general information, not individual advice.

## Editorial decisions

- Replaced toy counts with sourced lengths, dates, time ranges, percentages and grams.
- Used near-miss dates, confused mechanisms and overbroad claims as distractors. Each wrong-option feedback names its particular error.
- Kept the historical limits visible: the Indus script is undeciphered; the Great Bath’s exact use is uncertain; zero developed over time.
- Framed health guidance as general information. The topics give no diagnosis, dose, supplement advice or weight target.
- The fact-check file records every revised source-bearing field and the rejected claims that were dropped or narrowed.
